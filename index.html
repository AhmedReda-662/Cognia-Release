Local-first · Offline · No telemetry

Your notes, on your machine.

Cognia is a desktop knowledge workspace for studying and writing. Your work lives in a local database on your computer — no account, no cloud sync, no tracking.

Everything a study workspace needs

Writing editor

Headings, lists, task lists, tables, code blocks with highlighting, quotes, links, and KaTeX math — inline and block. What you write is stored as structured content and exported as clean Markdown.

Whiteboard per note

Freedraw, shapes, text, and images on an infinite canvas, side by side with your text in split view. Boards save automatically as editable data — never screenshots.

Workspaces & folders

Organize notes in nested folders across workspaces, with move, rename, search filtering, and delete confirmations that tell you exactly what will be removed.

Local attachments

Paste, drop, or pick images into notes. Files live beside the database on your disk, deduplicated by content, and travel with portable exports.

Honest autosave

Debounced saves with version guards: concurrent edits are refused instead of silently overwritten, and conflicts offer explicit Reload or Overwrite choices.

Calm by default

Light and dark themes, resizable split view, keyboard shortcuts, and a quiet interface that stays out of the way of your thinking.

Boards you can keep editing

Each note has its own whiteboard. Draw a diagram next to the paragraph that explains it, then reopen it months later and keep drawing — every stroke is preserved as editable objects.

  • Freedraw, shapes, arrows, text, image pasting, zoom and pan
  • Undo/redo, dark-mode aware, handwriting fonts work offline
  • Survives restarts, renames, and moves; per-note version protection

Your data stays portable

Export any note as a Markdown folder or a single ZIP: the text, the images, and the editable whiteboard scene travel together. Import a package back into any note — existing boards are never silently overwritten.
